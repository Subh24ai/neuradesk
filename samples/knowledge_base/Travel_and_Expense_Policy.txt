Northwind Labs — Travel and Expense Policy

Version 1.8 · Owner: Finance · Applies to all employees

Booking business travel

Book all flights and hotels through TravelPerk at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Trips outside your home country need approval from your manager before booking.

Hotel and meal limits

Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night, or 250 USD per night in London, New York and Tokyo. The daily meal allowance is 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

Submitting expense claims

Submit expense claims in Expensify within 30 days of the expense, with an itemised receipt for anything over 25 USD. Your manager approves claims, and approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

Company credit cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may request a company credit card from Finance. Personal purchases on a company card are not allowed and must be repaid within 7 days.

Mileage

Business use of a personal car is reimbursed at 0.58 USD per mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursed.
