# Northwind Labs — Leave and Time-Off Policy

Version 3.2 · Owner: People Team · Applies to all full-time employees

## Annual leave entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 26 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly at 2.17 days per month.

## How to request annual leave

Request annual leave in PeopleHub under Time Off → New Request at least 10 working days before your first day off. Your line manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Requests of 5 or fewer days made with less notice may still be approved at your manager's discretion.

## Carry-over and unused leave

You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they expire. Unused leave is not paid out, except when you leave the company.

## Sick leave

You receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are unwell and log the absence in PeopleHub under Time Off → Sick Day. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth and adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Notify the People Team at least 8 weeks before your intended start date by emailing people@northwind-labs.example.

## Bereavement and compassionate leave

You may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 paid days for an extended family member. No advance notice is required; inform your manager as soon as you reasonably can.

## Public holidays

The company observes 9 public holidays per year. The current year's list is published in PeopleHub under Company → Holiday Calendar. If you are required to work on a public holiday, you receive a replacement day off within 30 days.
