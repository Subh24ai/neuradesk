# Northwind Labs — IT Security and Access Policy

Version 4.0 · Owner: IT Security · Applies to all employees and contractors

## Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of your last 8 passwords. Passwords expire every 180 days; NeuraDesk emails you a reminder 14 days before expiry. Never share your password, including with IT staff — IT will never ask for it.

## Resetting a forgotten password

If you forget your password or are locked out, submit a NeuraDesk ticket such as "I forgot my password". A temporary password is issued automatically and must be changed at your next sign-in. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 15 minutes.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Authy app is mandatory for email, PeopleHub and GlobalConnect VPN. If you lose your phone, contact the IT Service Desk on extension 4400 to have MFA reset after an identity check.

## Requesting access to systems and shared drives

Request access to a system or shared drive through a NeuraDesk ticket that names the resource, for example "I need access to the Finance shared drive". Standard access is provisioned automatically. Access to production databases and payroll data also requires approval from the data owner and is reviewed every 90 days.

## Removing access and offboarding

Access is removed on an employee's last working day. Removing your own access, locking or deleting an account is never done automatically — NeuraDesk always asks you to confirm first. Requests to change another person's account must come from their manager and are handled by IT staff.

## Approved software

Only software on the Approved Software List may be installed. Request new software through a NeuraDesk ticket; your manager is notified and must approve it before IT installs it. Personal cloud storage such as personal Dropbox accounts must not be used for company data.

## Reporting security incidents

Report a suspected phishing email, lost laptop or data leak immediately through NeuraDesk or by calling the security hotline on extension 4911. Do not delete the suspicious email; forward it as an attachment to security@northwind-labs.example.
