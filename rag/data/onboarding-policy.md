# New Employee Onboarding Policy

## Overview
This policy describes the onboarding process for all new full-time employees. Onboarding spans the first 90 days of employment and is designed to ensure new employees have the tools, access, and context needed to become productive members of the organization. Responsibilities are shared between IT, HR, and the hiring manager.

## Pre-Start Preparations (IT)
IT provisions the new employee's laptop, badge, and Active Directory account three business days before the start date. Standard software (Microsoft 365, Slack, Zoom, VPN client) is pre-installed. The employee's corporate email account and Slack workspace access are activated on the morning of day one. ITSM creates a checklist ticket assigned to IT and the hiring manager tracking all provisioning tasks.

## Day One Checklist
The new employee collects their laptop and building access badge from the IT Help Desk on arrival. HR conducts a 60-minute orientation covering company policies, benefits enrollment, and the code of conduct. IT runs a 30-minute session covering VPN setup, password management, and security awareness. The hiring manager hosts a team introduction meeting and outlines the 30-60-90 day plan. The employee completes mandatory compliance training (data privacy, security awareness, workplace conduct) within the first week.

## Access and Systems Setup
Role-based access is provisioned by IT based on the job profile stored in the HR system. Access to specific tools, repositories, and data systems beyond the standard profile must be requested through the ITSM access provisioning workflow with manager approval. Contractors and fixed-term employees receive access scoped to their contract duration; accounts expire automatically on the contract end date.

## 30-60-90 Day Milestones
At 30 days, the hiring manager reviews initial progress and confirms all access requirements are met. At 60 days, the employee completes a self-assessment and receives structured feedback from the manager and key stakeholders. At 90 days, a formal review determines whether the employee has successfully completed the probationary period. Concerns identified during the 90-day period are documented in the HR system and addressed through a performance improvement plan if necessary.
