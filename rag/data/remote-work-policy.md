# Remote Work Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes guidelines for employees who work remotely, whether fully remote or in a hybrid arrangement. It covers eligibility, equipment, security requirements, and expectations for availability and communication. All remote work arrangements must be agreed in writing between the employee and their manager and documented in the HR system.

## Eligibility and Approval
Remote and hybrid work is available for roles that do not require physical presence for operational reasons. Employees must have completed their 90-day probationary period before being eligible for a remote arrangement. Requests are submitted via the HR portal and require manager approval followed by HR validation against role requirements. Fully remote arrangements for employees outside a 50-mile radius of a corporate office require VP-level approval.

## Security Requirements for Remote Work
All remote employees must connect to corporate systems via the approved VPN client (Cisco AnyConnect) at all times. Company data must never be stored on personal devices or unencrypted external storage. The home office must have a private, password-protected Wi-Fi network; public Wi-Fi usage requires VPN connection. Screen locking must be configured to activate after 5 minutes of inactivity. Employees are responsible for reporting any suspected security incidents — such as unauthorized access to their home network — to the Security team immediately.

## Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a laptop and necessary peripherals for remote employees. Additional equipment (external monitors, ergonomic chairs, standing desks) may be requested through the equipment request process and is subject to a per-employee budget of $1,500 every three years. Internet and phone expenses are the employee's responsibility unless the employee is classified as fully remote with no local office; in that case, a monthly internet stipend of $60 is reimbursable.

## Availability and Communication Expectations
Remote employees must be available during core hours (10:00–15:00 in their local time zone) and must keep their Slack status current. Video cameras must be enabled during team meetings and client calls unless the employee is in transit. Response time expectations for messages are the same as for on-site employees: Slack within 2 hours, email within one business day. Persistent availability issues are addressed through the standard performance management process.
