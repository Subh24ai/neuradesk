# Equipment Request and Management Policy

## Overview
This policy describes how employees request, receive, use, and return corporate equipment. All hardware assigned to employees is company property and must be used in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy. Equipment requests are managed through the IT Self-Service Portal and fulfilled by the IT Procurement team.

## Requesting Equipment
Submit an equipment request through the IT Self-Service Portal under Hardware Requests. Specify the item type, model preference if applicable, and business justification. Requests require manager approval. Standard items (replacement laptop charger, USB hub, webcam) are approved automatically and fulfilled within 2 business days. Non-standard items exceeding $500 require Director-level approval. Replacement laptops due to failure are treated as priority tickets and fulfilled within 1 business day.

## Standard Equipment Allocation
Each full-time employee receives a corporate laptop (MacBook Pro 14" for engineers, Surface Pro for business roles), a webcam, a headset, and a laptop bag at the time of onboarding. Remote employees additionally receive an external monitor and docking station. Hot-desk monitors and peripherals are available in all corporate offices for employees who work on-site regularly. Equipment specifications are reviewed annually and updated to reflect current procurement contracts.

## Equipment Care and Loss Reporting
Employees are responsible for reasonable care of assigned equipment. Physical damage caused by negligence may result in a cost recovery charge assessed by HR. Loss or theft must be reported to IT Security within 2 hours of discovery; IT will remotely wipe the device and issue a replacement. Employees must not tamper with, modify, or repair corporate hardware. Unauthorized modifications void the warranty and may trigger a security review.

## Equipment Return
All company equipment must be returned on or before the employee's last working day. IT coordinates equipment collection during the offboarding process. Unreturned equipment is considered company property loss and may be deducted from the final paycheck as permitted by applicable law. Returned equipment is wiped, inspected, and either reissued to the next employee or disposed of according to the e-waste policy.
