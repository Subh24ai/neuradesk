# Employee Offboarding Policy

## Overview
This policy covers the procedures for managing the departure of employees, whether through resignation, redundancy, retirement, or termination. A complete and timely offboarding process is critical for data security, license compliance, and legal obligations. HR initiates the offboarding workflow in the HR system no later than the day the departure is confirmed.

## Notice Period and Final Day
The standard notice period is four weeks for individual contributors and eight weeks for managers and above. The employee's last working day is recorded in the HR system and triggers all downstream offboarding automations. Employees on garden leave retain system access only to tools required to hand over work; all other access is suspended on the first day of garden leave.

## Access Revocation and Account Deactivation
All system access is revoked automatically within one hour of the offboarding event being confirmed in the HR system. This includes Active Directory account deactivation, removal from all security groups, revocation of cloud IAM roles, and deactivation of SaaS application accounts. VPN certificates are revoked and remote access sessions are terminated immediately. Access revocation is a destructive action and requires confirmation from the employee's manager before the automated workflow is triggered in cases of involuntary termination.

## Data and Knowledge Transfer
The departing employee must complete a knowledge transfer checklist, reviewed and signed off by their manager, at least five business days before their final day. Work-in-progress documentation should be migrated to shared team repositories. Personal files on corporate devices are deleted at the time of equipment collection; employees must ensure any business-relevant files are in shared drives before departure. Email and Slack data is retained for 90 days post-departure for compliance purposes and accessible to the manager with HR approval.

## Equipment Return and Final Payroll
The employee returns all corporate equipment to IT on or before the final working day. HR issues the final paycheck including accrued but unused PTO as required by applicable law. Any outstanding expense claims must be submitted within 10 business days of the final day. Benefits coverage ends at the end of the month of departure; COBRA or equivalent continuation coverage information is provided by HR within five business days.
