# Employee Leave Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines the types of leave available to full-time employees, the accrual rules, the approval process, and the procedures for requesting and recording leave. All leave requests must be submitted through the HR Self-Service Portal. Employees are responsible for keeping their leave balances accurate and ensuring adequate coverage is arranged before taking time off.

## Leave Types and Accrual
Annual Leave (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days per month (20 days per year) from their start date. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 30 days; balances above 30 days are forfeited at year-end. Sick Leave: 10 days per year provided upfront on January 1; unused sick leave does not carry over. Parental Leave: 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 4 weeks for secondary caregivers, available after 6 months of employment. Bereavement Leave: up to 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family; requires manager notification.

## Requesting Annual Leave
Submit leave requests through the HR Self-Service Portal at least 5 business days in advance for absences of 3 days or fewer, and at least 10 business days in advance for longer periods. The request is routed to your direct manager for approval. Managers must respond within 2 business days. During blackout periods (Q4 fiscal year-end and product launch windows), leave requests require Director-level approval. Emergency leave with less than 24 hours notice must be communicated directly to your manager by phone or Slack.

## Sick Leave Procedures
Notify your manager and team at the start of the working day if you are too unwell to work. Record sick leave in the HR portal upon your return. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note or medical certificate is required. Sick leave may not be used for planned medical appointments that can be scheduled outside working hours. Abuse of sick leave is subject to HR review and may result in disciplinary action.

## Leave Approval and Conflicts
Managers approve or decline leave requests based on team capacity and project commitments. When multiple team members request overlapping leave, priority is given to requests submitted first. Managers may ask employees to reschedule non-urgent leave to ensure adequate team coverage. Approved leave may be cancelled in exceptional business circumstances with at least 5 business days notice and documented justification. Disputes about leave decisions should be escalated to HR through the employee relations process.
